# WARDOGS map banners — active source register

Three actual map previews are now installed in `docs/assets/map-banners/`, and
`manifest.json` maps Bakurani, Ozeti and Zestafona to their exact local assets.

The files are the official RCON's DayStartClear 1280×720 previews,
mirrored by WARCON's public WARDOGS server-administration repository.
See `docs/assets/map-banners/ATTRIBUTION.md` for provenance and rights.
They are © BULKHEAD / WARDOGS, not MIT-licensed source art.

The current-map and last-played-map tiles, home match cards,
per-player map breakdown and Discord recap renderer share the same
local manifest. Unknown or unavailable maps fall back to text.

Map names and source IDs: Bakurani=Kavkazi, Ozeti=Europe,
Zestafona=NorthAmerica. Do not infer map rotation or match results from art.

The collector's official server status and saved public WARCON snapshots
remain the source of actual map names, scores and player statistics.
