## Sunday report schedule

- **Target post:** Sunday at 12:00 PM **America/New_York**, including daylight saving changes; scheduled runs can be delayed, so include retry/recovery and exactly-one intended weekly-report message. Do not claim minute-exact delivery.
- **Window:** previous Sunday noon ET inclusive through this Sunday noon ET exclusive, using timezone-aware start/end converted to UTC. Do not silently use UTC calendar weeks; this also means a report may cross a WARDOGS season boundary.
- **Season separation:** WARCON's official season 2 is `wardogs-2`, beginning **2026-10-15T16:00:00Z**. Read official season metadata as the authority; assign matches/observations to actual season by occurrence time and never add Season 2 totals to Season 3. WARCON's public board accepts `range=s:wardogs-2`. The production 1ADAAR leaderboard is **all-time**, so **Do not** label it as current-season data. At a season crossover, segment weeks by actual official season and avoid mixed-season award winners.
- **Archive path:** `docs/data/weekly/<official-season-key>/<YYYY-MM-DD>.json` with explicit `fromUtc`, `toUtc`, `seasonKey`, metric source, coverage flags and provenance. Permanently retain and serve compact summaries, not kill-feed logs or raw identities.
- **Repeat-post protection:** a persisted delivery checkpoint is keyed by the week-ending date. One Discord message covers all season segments in that week. An uncertain Discord network timeout or a failed Git commit can still cause an ambiguous outcome; do not claim mathematically guaranteed exactly-once delivery.
- **No activity:** do not spam the recap channel with invented weekly winners. Show a neutral archive empty-state or omit posting until a qualified match exists.

## Report numbers and limits

**Eligible from completed match data**: weekly completed matches, unique pseudonymized player participation (from match lines, *not* global Steam identifiers), most match appearances, most wins, kills, defeats, map most played, match participation minutes per faction, faction **appearances** (do not mislabel them exact join counts), faction wins/rates with minimum sample counts, repeat players where the archive covers both weeks.

**Conditional on WARCON's actual verified kill-feed coverage**: most headshots, longest shot, streaks and combat awards. Never display unverified zero as proof of no headshots. Name source and coverage; null/unavailable is better than false precision.

**Season-to-date awards from `range=s:<official season key>`**: best K/D only for players with **more than 20 completed matches *during the season*** (not all-time); disclose threshold. Full seasonal board pages may cap 1,000 players and represent partial coverage. Avoid computing per-week seasonal differences from two all-time snapshots.

**Unavailable until a verified source exists**: highest/lowest character level with kills, precise faction-switch/join events and exact time after switching, weapon-specific TTK, true spawn-to-death lifetime. WARCON player **rank** is leaderboard placement, not in-game character level.

**Visual hierarchy**: 1AD logo > community server name > `WEEKLY INTELLIGENCE` > official Season # > week ending date > (i) server activity, (ii) selected player awards, (iii) faction distribution, (iv) one source-of-truth link to permanent report. Shareable, readable on mobile and free of pressure to seed.
