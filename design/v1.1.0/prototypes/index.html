How to submit a design:

Commit `prototypes/{screen}.html`, its CSS/JS, matching `screenshots/{screen}-390.png` and `screenshots/{screen}-1440.png`, plus a component spec. Then update screen-manifest.json to link the exact files. Screenshots alone do not count as editable implementation instructions.

Source rules: original official 1AD, WARDOGS and faction assets remain unchanged. No fictional live players, invented scores, Steam IDs or secrets. This workspace does not deploy or post to Discord.
